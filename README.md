I am an infectious disease epidemiology researcher with interests in antimicrobial resistance (AMR), bacterial genomics, and One Health. I combine both wet and dry lab (computational approaches) to understand bacteria that are drug resistant and their implications for health. 
Languages: R, Python, Bash
Genomics: assembly, pan-genome, phylogenetics, AMR/MGE/virulence profiling
Spatial: Moran’s I, LISA hotspot mapping
My long-term goal is to integrate genomic, epidemiological, and spatial data to investigate the emergence and spread of antimicrobial resistance.
